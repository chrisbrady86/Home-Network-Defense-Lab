# Wireless Testing — Scope and Authorization

## Authorization

All wireless testing in this lab is conducted against access points and client devices I personally own, on frequencies and at power levels within FCC limits, in a physically isolated environment.

No testing is performed against any network, device, or signal I do not own.

## Scope

In scope:
- Access points purchased for the lab
- Client devices purchased for the lab
- Traffic generated by those devices

Out of scope:
- Any neighboring network, whether or not it is in range
- Any device not owned by me
- Any production network

## Publication

Captures, methodology, and tooling notes are kept offline. This repository records that the work was scoped and authorized, not how it was carried out.
